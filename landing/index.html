Questions

Before you download.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no real money, no wagering, and nothing to win. It is a training app: you are answering questions about poker decisions, the same way a chess puzzle app works.

Where do the answers come from?

Established preflop charts for 100bb cash games, 6-max and 8-max. Every hand shows the frequency mix and the reasoning, and you can open the full range chart the spot came from — so you can check the logic rather than memorise a grid.

Why does it sometimes say "close enough"?

Because a hand the chart raises 54% and folds 46% of the time does not have a right answer. An action within ten points of the most frequent one counts, and the panel names the tie rather than taking credit for a coin toss it happened to win.

What exactly is free?

50 hands to start, and 20 new hands every day after that, forever — no account and no card. Your streak, XP, levels, badges, overall accuracy and every explanation you have already unlocked stay free too. Pro adds unlimited hands, your accuracy at every position with the ability to drill any of them, and your full hand history.

I already use a solver. Is this for me?

As a supplement, not a replacement. A solver is for deep study at a desk. This is for the minutes you'd otherwise spend scrolling, keeping preflop sharp between sessions — and it computes no equity or EV of its own, so it is not pretending to be one.
