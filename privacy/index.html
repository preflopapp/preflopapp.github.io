Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026
Last updated: 25 September 2026

Preflop does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app records stays on your device, in the app's own private storage. It is never transmitted. That includes:

  • Your practice history — the hands you were dealt, the action you chose, whether it was right, how long you took, and the position, situation, stack depth and table size each hand was played at.
  • Your progress — streak, XP, level, daily goal, personal records, and which badges you have earned.
  • Your settings — appearance, card faces and backs, animation speed, sound, haptics and background motion, practice filters, the hand timer, your daily goal, and whether reminders are on.
  • Your answers to the welcome questions — the format you play, your level, how sure you are of your charts, and what you want to improve. They shape the app for you and are never sent anywhere.

The Home Screen widget shows your streak, your remaining free hands and a hand to answer. It reads them from storage shared only between Preflop and its own widget, on the same device.

If you turn on practice reminders, they are scheduled by iOS on your device. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

If you have device backups turned on, this data may be included in your device's backup along with the rest of your apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, an email address, or any sign-up.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

The app does not ask for permission to track you across other apps or websites, because it does not do so.

Purchases

Preflop Pro is sold through the App Store. Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple, and we never see or receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a valid purchase or subscription exists. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy, and your subscription is managed in your App Store account settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no messaging, and no way to share anything with other users.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes everything it has stored, including your entire practice history and progress. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and nothing to request from us.

To clear your history and progress without deleting the app, use Settings → Help → Reset Progress. It deletes your hand history, XP, level, streak and badges, and keeps your plan and purchases.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy information will be updated to match.
