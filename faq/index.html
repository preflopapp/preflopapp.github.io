Frequently asked questions

Is Preflop free?

Yes. You start with 50 hands, then get 20 more every day, free forever. Your streak, XP, levels, badges, overall accuracy and every explanation you have already seen stay free too.

What does Preflop Pro add?

Unlimited hands with no daily cap, your accuracy at every position with the ability to drill any of them, and your full hand history with every explanation.

How much does Pro cost?

In the US, $11.99 a month with a 7‑day free trial, or $59.99 once for Lifetime. The App Store shows the price for your country before you buy. Purchases are made in the app; there is nothing to buy on this site.

Monthly or Lifetime — what's the difference?

Monthly is a subscription that renews each month and includes a 7‑day free trial for new subscribers. Lifetime is a one-time purchase: pay once and Pro is yours for good, with nothing to renew. The free trial is on the monthly plan only, because Apple does not offer trials on one-time purchases.

I bought Lifetime but I'm still being charged monthly.

Apple treats them as two separate purchases, so buying Lifetime does not cancel the monthly subscription. Cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Preflop, and tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings → Help.

Which spots does it cover?

Raise first in from every seat, facing an open from every seat against every opener, and facing a 3-bet after you opened — at 6-max and 8-max tables, 100bb deep.

Where do the answers come from?

Every spot is drawn from a fixed preflop range chart for that seat, situation and table size. After you answer you see the full frequency mix for your hand and can open the chart it came from.

Why does it sometimes say “Close enough”?

Because a hand the chart raises 54% of the time and folds 46% does not have one right answer. An action within ten points of the most frequent one is graded Close, and so is any action the chart takes at least 15% of the time. A Close answer counts toward your streak, XP and accuracy, and the explanation tells you which action the chart takes most often.

Can I choose what to practise?

Yes. Settings → Practice → Practice Filters narrows the deal by category, table size and your seat. New categories unlock as you level up. With Pro you can also pick any position from your stats and drill it on its own.

I already use a solver. Is this for me?

As a supplement, not a replacement. A solver is for deep study at a desk. Preflop is for the few minutes between sessions, keeping your preflop sharp. It computes no equity or EV of its own.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Preflop is made for iPhone; there is no iPad or Android version.

Is there a widget, or reminders?

Both, and both optional. The Home Screen widget comes in three sizes and shows your streak; the larger two also deal you a hand that opens straight into practice. Practice reminders are off until you allow them, and you can change them in Settings → Notifications. They are scheduled on your iPhone, not sent from a server.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Help → Reset Progress deletes your hand history, XP, level, streak and badges. Your plan and purchases are kept. It cannot be undone.

Do I need an account? What data do you collect?

No account, and nothing. Everything stays on your device — no analytics, no ads, no network calls. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this gambling?

No. Preflop is a training app. There is no real money, no wagering, and nothing to win.

How do I get help?

Email support@preflopapp.com, or see the Support page.
