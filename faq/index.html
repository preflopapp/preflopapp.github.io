Frequently asked questions

Is Preflop free?

Yes. You start with 50 hands, then get 20 more every day, free forever. Your streak, XP, levels, badges, overall accuracy and every explanation you have already seen stay free too.

What does Preflop Pro add?

Unlimited hands with no daily cap, your accuracy at every position with the ability to drill any of them, and your full hand history with every explanation.

Monthly or Lifetime — what's the difference?

Monthly is a subscription that renews each month and includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Lifetime is a one-time purchase: pay once and Pro is yours for good, with nothing to renew. The prices for your country are shown in the app before you buy.

I bought Lifetime but I'm still being charged monthly.

Apple treats them as two separate purchases, so buying Lifetime does not cancel the monthly subscription. Cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Preflop, and tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or under Settings → Subscription.

Which spots does it cover?

Raise first in from every seat, facing an open from every seat against every opener, and facing a 3-bet after you opened — at 6-max and 8-max tables, 100bb deep.

Where do the answers come from?

Every spot is drawn from a fixed preflop range chart for that seat, situation and table size. After you answer you see the full frequency mix for your hand and can open the chart it came from.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Do I need an account? What data do you collect?

No account, and nothing. Everything stays on your device — no analytics, no ads, no network calls. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this gambling?

No. Preflop is a training app. There is no real money, no wagering, and nothing to win.

How do I get help?

Email support@preflopapp.com, or see the Support page.
