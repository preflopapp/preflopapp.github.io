Subscriptions and purchases

Preflop Pro is available as a monthly auto-renewing subscription (existing annual subscribers keep theirs), or as a one-time Lifetime purchase, all sold through the App Store. A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A training app

Preflop is for practice and education. There is no real money, no wagering, and nothing to win. Nothing in the app is a guarantee of results at a real table.
