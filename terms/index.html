Subscriptions and purchases

Preflop Pro is available as a monthly auto-renewing subscription or as a one-time Lifetime purchase, both sold through the App Store. A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you subscribe. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
