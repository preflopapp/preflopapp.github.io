Why

FAQ

Before you download.

More answers on the full FAQ.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no real money, no wagering and nothing to win. It is a training app, the way a chess puzzle app is.

Where do the answers come from?

Every spot is drawn from a fixed preflop range chart for that seat, situation and table size. After you answer you see the full frequency mix for your hand and can open the chart it came from.

Why does it sometimes say “Mixed”?

Because a hand the chart raises 54% of the time and folds 46% does not have one right answer. An action within ten points of the most frequent one, or any action the chart takes at least a quarter of the time, is graded Mixed. It counts as correct for your streak, XP and accuracy, and the explanation names the action the chart takes most often.

What exactly is free?

20 hands every day, with the answer, the chart’s mix and the reason after every hand. Your streak, XP, levels and badges are free too. Pro adds unlimited hands, your stats, your dashboard, Practice Filters, your full hand history and a daily goal.

Do I need an account?

No. Your practice stays on your device, with no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The only thing we receive is feedback you choose to send.

I already use a solver. Is this for me?

As a supplement, not a replacement. A solver is for deep study at a desk. Preflop is for the few minutes between sessions, keeping your preflop sharp. It computes no equity or EV of its own.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. There is no iPad or Android version.
