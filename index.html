Preflop deals you real spots, one hand at a time, and asks the only question that matters: what do you do here? Then it tells you why.

Every hand, explained

Answer, and you get the correct action with the reasoning behind it — not just a verdict. See the exact frequency mix for your hand, and open the full range chart the spot came from to see where it sits among the hands around it.

The spots you actually play

  • Raise first in, from every seat
  • Facing an open, from every seat against every opener
  • Facing a 3-bet after you opened
  • 6-max and 8-max, 100bb deep

Find the leak

Preflop tracks your accuracy seat by seat and situation by situation, then points you at the one costing you the most and lets you drill it on its own until it stops being a leak.

Review every hand

Your whole history stays searchable — the cards, the seat, what you did, whether it was right, and the explanation. Filter to the ones you got wrong and put them back on the table.
