Preflop Trainer

Practise preflop poker decisions, hand by hand, on iPhone.

Preflop Trainer deals you a spot, you pick an action, and it tells you what the solver does and why. It tracks your accuracy by position so you can see where your game actually leaks.

It is a training app. There is no real-money play, no wagering, and no betting of any kind.

No accounts and no tracking: everything the app records stays on your device.
