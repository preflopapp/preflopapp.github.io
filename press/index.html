Press kit

In one line

Preflop is an iPhone app that deals you real preflop poker spots and explains the right answer to every one.

In a paragraph

Preflop is where most of a poker player's money is decided, and it is the one street that can actually be learned by heart. Preflop deals you real spots — raise first in, facing an open, facing a 3-bet, at 6-max and 8-max tables, 100bb deep — and after every answer shows the correct action, the reasoning, the exact frequency mix and the range chart it came from. It tracks accuracy seat by seat, points at the leak costing you the most, and lets you drill it. There is no account and nothing leaves the device.
