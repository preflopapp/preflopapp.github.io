From PreflopMotion.swift, as --dur-* and --ease-* in tokens.css. Animate transform and opacity only. Reduce Motion: durations go to zero, GSAP does not run, content just appears.

Rules

  • No gradients. No dashed or dotted lines.
  • Two ambers: --accent-fill behind a label, --accent for text and strokes.
  • Text on a panel is --text, never a fixed white.
  • Green and red mean right and wrong, or raise and fold. Not chrome.
  • Content and the primary CTA are in the HTML. Nothing waits on JS.
  • No inline scripts or styles. No requests leave the origin.
Not linked, not indexed, not in the sitemap.
